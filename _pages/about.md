I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at **Sejong University**. Before joining Sejong University in September 2026, I spent over a decade at the **Bank of Korea**, most recently as a Senior Economist, where I also provided strategic advisory support for the BIS Committee on the Global Financial System (CGFS).

I received my PhD in Economics from **University College London (UCL)** in 2020.

## Research Interests

My research lies in macroeconomics, with a focus on:

* Monetary policy and its transmission mechanism
* Heterogeneous-agent macroeconomics (HANK/TANK models)
* Financial frictions and business cycles
* Household inequality and open-economy macroeconomics

## Selected Publications

* **Financial Frictions: Micro vs Macro Volatility** (with R. Faccini, R. Luetticke, M. O. Ravn, and T. Renkin), *American Economic Review*, 2026, 116(2), 464–501.
* **Optimal Monetary Policy with Search Frictions and Consumption Inequality**, *Economic Modelling*, 2026, 160, 107619.
