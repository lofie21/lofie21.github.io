excerpt: 'Working paper. In a two-agent New Keynesian model where hand-to-mouth households hold nominal debt, all five monetary transmission channels of Auclert (2019) admit closed-form expressions. Two structural restrictions follow: the earnings heterogeneity channel is a fixed multiple of real marginal cost, so its ratio to inflation is invariant to the wage schedule; and the interest rate exposure and Fisher channels are dual, both deriving from the same hand-to-mouth balance-sheet position.'
